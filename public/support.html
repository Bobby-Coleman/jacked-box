Help & contact

How do I start a party?

Open RiffRaff and tap Start a party. Your phone shows a 4-letter room code and a QR code. Friends join with the code in the RiffRaff app or at the website. Nobody needs an account to join.

Which phone plays the sound?

One phone is the room speaker (the first one by default). It plays the host's voice and the music. Tap Tap to turn on sound on that phone. Anyone can make their phone the speaker from the room menu.

Someone's phone fell asleep

No problem. When they open RiffRaff again they rejoin the same seat. If the hosting phone drops, another phone takes over automatically within a few seconds.

What does Premium include?

The classic games are free. Premium unlocks every other game, including all the face games. One Premium player in the room unlocks Premium games for everyone there.

Restore a subscription on a new phone

Open Account and tap Restore purchases, or sign in with the account you used when you subscribed.

Cancel a subscription

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RiffRaff. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RiffRaff.

Delete your account

Open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and profile.

Report a problem or abuse

Hosts can remove a player from the room at any time. To report abuse, a bug, or anything else, contact us below and include the room code and time if you can.
